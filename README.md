# Drivers-of-AI-Adoption-in-EU-Enterprises

### Drivers of Artificial Intelligence Adoption in European Union Enterprises

Analyzed enterprise AI adoption across the 27 EU member states using 2024 Eurostat data and multivariate statistical methods. Applied factor analysis to identify latent dimensions underlying AI adoption, digital participation, ICT workforce conditions, employment shortages, and digital & energy investment. Hierarchical and non-hierarchical clustering were then used to group countries according to their digital and socioeconomic profiles.

The analysis identified distinct country profiles and explored how enterprise AI adoption relates to broader digital, labour-market, and investment characteristics.
